Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 11, 2026 • Effective Immediately

1. About Meetrix

Meetrix is an independent, open-source video conferencing web application designed to facilitate real-time peer-to-peer audio and video communication, AI-assisted transcription, and meeting engagement analytics.

2. Data We Collect and Process

Meetrix collects only the data necessary to provide meeting room functionality and user accounts:

  • Account Information: When you register an account, we collect your full name, email address, and password. Passwords are cryptographically hashed using bcryptjs (with 12 salt rounds) prior to storage in our database. We never store, log, or have access to plaintext passwords.
  • Meeting Identifiers & Metadata: When a meeting is created, we generate an alphanumeric meeting identifier (e.g., ABC-1X2), record meeting titles, host identifier, participant list, start time, end time, and duration.
  • Audio & Video Streams (WebRTC): Live audio and video are transmitted directly between participants peer-to-peer using WebRTC technology. Raw audio and video streams are not recorded or saved on our servers. Public Google STUN servers (stun:stun.l.google.com:19302) are utilized solely to establish initial peer-to-peer network routing.
  • Transcription & Meeting Summaries: When the transcription feature is enabled, speech is transcribed in the browser using the Web Speech API, or processed via temporary audio buffer uploads to the Hugging Face Inference API. Any temporary audio files generated on our server are permanently deleted immediately upon transcription completion (via fs.unlinkSync). Resulting text transcripts and AI-generated meeting summaries are associated with the meeting record.
  • Engagement Metrics: The client-side application analyzes microphone activity and participant participation state to compute anonymized engagement and speaking-time statistics.

3. Third-Party Services

Meetrix interfaces with the following third-party infrastructure providers:

  • MongoDB Atlas: Cloud database provider for persistent storage of account records, meeting metadata, transcripts, and engagement statistics.
  • Hugging Face Inference API: Used for processing speech-to-text transcription (Whisper model) and meeting summaries (BART model).
  • Google STUN Servers: Used solely for WebRTC network NAT traversal.
  • Content Delivery Networks: Cloud CDNs (cdnjs, jsDelivr, socket.io) deliver frontend utility libraries.

Meetrix is an independent project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by OpenAI, Google LLC, Zoom Video Communications, or Hugging Face.

4. Data Retention and Auto-Deletion

Meeting records, transcripts, summaries, and associated engagement metrics are scheduled to auto-expire (default: 24 hours from creation) using automated database Time-To-Live (TTL) indexes. Expired meetings are purged automatically by the database engine.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

Meetrix uses browser localStorage solely to maintain your authentication session token (JSON Web Token) and meeting preferences. We do not use advertising, tracking, or third-party behavioral cookies.
